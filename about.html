About Maritza

Maritza Castro

From English Learner to Educational Leader

Maritza Castro's journey in education has been shaped by her own experiences as a student. Growing up as an English Language Learner, she understood what it felt like to be in a classroom without always feeling that she completely understood or belonged. Those experiences eventually inspired her to pursue a career in education, particularly working with bilingual students.

Maritza began her career as a fourth-grade dual language teacher on the Spanish side of the program. Her first year helped her develop skills in classroom management, lesson planning, working with families, and understanding the kind of educator she wanted to become.

As her career progressed, Maritza moved from supporting students within her own classroom to serving as a Dual Language Lead. In this role, she works across grade levels, supports teachers, assists with curriculum and instruction, and helps guide professional learning communities.

Her commitment to education has also led her to pursue a doctorate in education. She is particularly interested in how schools can better support bilingual students, educators, and families.

Becoming a Dual Language Lead is one of Maritza's proudest accomplishments. Her journey from learning English as a student, to becoming a bilingual teacher, and eventually supporting other educators brings her career full circle.
