From English Learner to Educational Leader

Maritza Castro's journey is a story of education, growth, and leadership. From her experience as an English Language Learner to becoming a bilingual educator and Dual Language Lead, she continues to use her experiences to support students, teachers, and families.

Her Journey

Education

Maritza's own experience as an English Language Learner inspired her to pursue a career helping bilingual students feel supported and understood in the classroom.

Leadership

After beginning her career as a fourth-grade dual language teacher, Maritza continued growing professionally and became a Dual Language Lead.

Continued Growth

Maritza is currently pursuing a doctorate in education while continuing to develop as an educational leader and advocate for bilingual education.
